# WavePoint images

The November 2026 images are real product captures, added under new filenames so the earlier assets remain intact.

- `homepage-2026-11.png` — WavePoint’s public homepage at https://wavepoint.space/, captured on November 6, 2026 at 1440 × 1000 after dismissing optional cookies.
- `day-chart-2026-11.png` — Day chart from a running iPhone simulator on November 6, 2026, at 1170 × 2532. Dark appearance; no personal birth-chart data.
- `journal-2026-11.png` — Journal from the same simulator session, with fictional sample Moments and media.

The iPhone images show a development preview. They are not evidence that these exact screens are distributed in TestFlight. The homepage caption makes that distinction; the app’s release status remains TestFlight.

Earlier `wavepoint-web.png` and `calendar-2027-august.jpg` files are retained as historical assets, but are no longer used in the active pages. The physical calendar and planner are no longer available for purchase.
