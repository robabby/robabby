# PartySlate interface examples

Historical images supplied from Rob Abby’s retained PartySlate archive and selected during the November 2026 case-study review. These are original archive captures or PDF page renders, not reconstructed interfaces.

- `buttons.jpg`: Product Day presentation, page 32, preserved in the September 2023 archive. A later design-system example, not the original 2019 audit.
- `component-process.jpg`: same presentation, page 33. Proposed component-development process; not evidence of uniform adoption.
- `find-a-venue.png`: early development prototype retained with later Next.js/SSR notes. Work in progress with an unfinished results area, not the finished interface or the original map launch.
- `onboarding-company.jpg`, `onboarding-event.jpg`, `onboarding-mobile.jpg`: Q1 2023 onboarding design document, pages 4, 18 and 65. Design artifacts, not production screenshots or evidence of measured conversion outcomes.

The original source documents and research remain in the private career archive. Public captions preserve the distinction between design, development and released work.
